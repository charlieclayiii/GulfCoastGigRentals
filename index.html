🏥 Extra Earning Opportunity — NEMT Trips

Gulf Coast Uber Rentals holds active contracts with ProCare, Medicaid, and Workers' Compensation transportation programs. Qualified drivers in our fleet can be dispatched on Non-Emergency Medical Transportation (NEMT) trips during off-peak hours — giving you a second income stream on top of your gig earnings.

NEMT trips pay $18–$35 each. During slow gig hours, our dispatchers route medical transport trips directly to you. Most competitors can't offer this — our contracts took years to build.
